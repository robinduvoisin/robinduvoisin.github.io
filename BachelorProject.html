Uncovering Sleep Modulation Effects through EEG signals

Supervisor
Manuel C. Dominguez, Dr. Lustenberger
Institution
Neural Control of Movement Lab at ETH Zurich

Abstract

My project was part of the research for the Sleeploop program jointly conducted by UZH and ETH, and it comprised two main components. In the first part, I designed a protocol for an experiment aimed at evaluating the impact of pre-sleep arousal levels on memory consolidation. The second part involved processing EEG data obtained from a finger-tapping task. I conducted an analysis of the dynamics of power spectral densities and correlated them with indicators of neuronal fatigue.
